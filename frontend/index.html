About RemAdvisor

RemAdvisor is a free rate comparison tool for the African and Asian diaspora. Whether you want to send money home or simply exchange currency at the best rate — we compare all major providers in seconds so you never leave money on the table.

💳 Send Money Home

Compare live remittance rates from Wise, Sendwave, Remitly, TapTap Send and more. We show the exact amount your recipient will receive after all fees — not just the headline rate. Best for GBP to NGN, USD to NGN, EUR to GHS, and all major corridors.

💱 Exchange Currency

Need to know the best USD to NGN rate today? Or compare GBP to GHS across providers? Our exchange rate comparison shows which provider gives the most Naira, Cedis or Shillings for your money — covering Wise, Revolut, XE Money, OFX and more.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best app to send money to Nigeria from the UK?
It changes daily. Wise, Sendwave, TapTap Send and Remitly all compete for the best GBP to NGN rate. Use RemAdvisor to compare live rates right now — the best app today may not be the best tomorrow.
What is the USD to NGN rate today?
The mid-market USD to NGN rate changes throughout the day. Use our Exchange tab to see today's live rate from multiple providers and find who gives the most Naira for your dollar.
What is the best way to send money to Ghana from the UK?
Wise and Sendwave are typically strong for GBP to GHS transfers. However rates change daily — always compare on RemAdvisor before sending to make sure you get the best deal.
Is RemAdvisor free to use?
Yes, 100% free. We earn a small commission when you click through and sign up to a provider — at no extra cost to you. This keeps the service free for everyone.
